Tips and help

Printing your personal timetable

What to look for

  1. My Details. Open it from the account menu.
  2. My Timetable. Opens the weekly timetable window.
  3. Print. Inside the timetable window; then use the browser print dialog.

Go to My Details → click My Timetable → click the Print button inside the timetable window.
