Getting started

How to sign in

  1. Open the portalGo to my-school-attendance-manager.replit.app in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
  2. Enter your school usernameThis is the username your line manager or IT Admin gave you (e.g. ben.obrien or maths).
  3. Enter your passwordUse the password provided to you. If you have forgotten it, contact Admin or IT.
  4. Click Sign InYou will be taken straight to your personalised homepage based on your role.
