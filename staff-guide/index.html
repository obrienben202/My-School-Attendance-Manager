Staff Guide — 2024 / 25

My School Attendance Manager

A complete staff portal for tracking attendance, managing courses, and viewing timetables — all in one place.

🌐 Portal address: my-school-attendance-manager.replit.app

Prepared for all teaching and support staff  ·  Confidential — internal use only

1   What is the Staff Portal?

The My School Attendance Manager is a secure, web-based portal that gives every member of staff a personalised view of their timetable, courses, and learner data. It connects directly to the school's Google Sheets data — so information is always up to date, refreshing automatically every 15 minutes.

There is nothing to install. Staff simply open a web browser on any device and go to the address above.

Portal address:  https://my-school-attendance-manager.replit.app
This is publicly accessible — staff can use it from home, on a mobile, or on any school device.

2   How to Sign In

Open the portal Go to my-school-attendance-manager.replit.app in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
Enter your school username This is the username your line manager or IT Admin gave you (e.g. ben.obrien or maths).
Enter your password Use the password provided to you. If you have forgotten it, contact Admin or IT.
Click Sign In You will be taken straight to your personalised homepage based on your role.

3   Staff Roles

Every staff member is assigned a role that controls what they can see and do in the portal.

Role Who has it Access level
Admin Senior leadership, IT administrators Full access — manage users, roles, all data, and sync settings
HR Human Resources staff Full access equivalent to Admin, including staff records
Head of Dept Heads of Department Department timetable, all courses, read-only user list; can edit course timetable slots
Staff Teaching and support staff Personal timetable, own course data, attendance marking, My Details
Office Office and admin staff Attendance overview, registers, reports — no course editing

4   Key Features

🏠   Homepage Dashboard

The homepage gives a quick snapshot the moment you log in — today's schedule, attendance headlines, upcoming lessons, and any at-risk learner alerts.

📅   My Timetable

From My Details, tap the My Timetable button to see a full Mon–Fri calendar view of your personal weekly schedule. Lessons are shown on a live time axis (08:30–15:30) so you can see exactly when each lesson falls. Shared Morning Meetings for Head of Department entries appear automatically for all HoD users.

📚   My Courses

View every course you teach. Click any course card to open the Course Detail page, which shows:

👥

Enrolled Learners

Full register of students on the course with attendance percentages, year group, and student reference numbers.

🕐

Timetable Slots

All scheduled lesson times for that course across the week. Admin and HoD can add, edit, or remove slots.

📋

Assessment Plan

Assessments linked to the course — type, due date, grading scheme, and mandatory status.

📊

Reports & Registers

Print or export the Course Register, Attendance Analysis, Completion Report, Retention Statistics, and Grade Distribution in one click.

🏫

Course Details

Level, Faculty, Academic Year, Course Hours, Status — all pulled automatically from the school's live Google Sheet.

📈

Progressions

Track individual learner progression routes and outcomes linked to each course.

✅   eRegisters & Attendance

Mark attendance for any lesson directly in the portal. The system records Present, Absent, and Late statuses and updates the learner's overall attendance percentage in real time. Learners below the target threshold are automatically highlighted as At Risk.

🏛️   Department Timetable (HoD & Admin only)

A full Mon–Fri grid showing every lesson across the department. Filter by individual staff member or view all at once. Shared Morning Meeting slots are highlighted in amber. The page refreshes automatically every 5 seconds.

👤   User Management (Admin & HR only)

Create, edit, and manage all staff accounts. Assign roles, reset passwords, and sync user data from the school's staff register. The list auto-refreshes every 5 seconds so changes are visible immediately.

🔄   Data Sync

The portal pulls live data from the school's Google Sheets every 15 minutes automatically. Admins can also trigger an instant sync at any time from the Data Sync page. The following data is kept in sync:

  • Course names, staff assignments, year groups, and enrolled student counts
  • Student roster (names, references, year groups, form groups)
  • Attendance records and KPI benchmarks

6   Helpful Tips

Accessing on mobile

The portal works on phones and tablets. On a mobile browser, tap Add to Home Screen (Safari on iPhone, or the browser menu on Android) to pin it like an app — no App Store needed.

Printing a register or curriculum sheet

Open the course, click Print Curriculum or a report name in the right-hand panel, and use your browser's print dialog. All reports are formatted for A4.

Printing your personal timetable

Go to My Details → click My Timetable → click the Print button inside the timetable window.

Data not showing the latest changes?

The portal syncs every 15 minutes. If you need it immediately, ask an Admin to run a manual sync from the Data Sync page.

Forgotten password or wrong username?

Contact your line manager or Admin Team. Credentials are managed centrally through the User Management section of the portal.
