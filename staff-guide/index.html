Getting started

  • What is the Staff Portal?What My School Attendance Manager is, what it connects to and its address.
  • How to sign inOpen the portal, enter your school username and password and sign in.
  • Staff rolesThe roles that control what each member of staff can see and do.

Courses

  • My CoursesView every course you teach and open a Course Detail page.
  • Enrolled LearnersThe course register of students with attendance percentages, year group and references.
  • Timetable SlotsScheduled lesson times for a course; Admin and HoD can add, edit or remove slots.
  • Assessment PlanAssessments linked to a course: type, due date, grading scheme and mandatory status.
  • Course reports and registersPrint or export the Course Register, Attendance Analysis, Completion, Retention and Grade Distribution.
  • Course DetailsLevel, Faculty, Academic Year, Course Hours and Status pulled from the school's Google Sheet.
  • ProgressionsLearner progression routes and outcomes linked to each course.

Daily work

  • Homepage dashboardThe snapshot you see after signing in: schedule, attendance headlines and alerts.
  • My TimetableYour personal Mon-Fri weekly calendar, opened from My Details.
  • eRegisters and attendanceMarking attendance for a lesson and how At Risk learners are highlighted.
  • Department TimetableMon-Fri grid of every lesson across the department (Head of Dept and Admin only).

Administration

  • User ManagementCreate, edit and manage staff accounts, roles and passwords (Admin and HR).
  • Data SyncHow the portal pulls data from Google Sheets every 15 minutes and what is synced.
  • What can each role do?Table of features by role: Admin/HR, Head of Dept, Staff and Office.

Tips and help

  • Accessing on mobileUse the portal on a phone or tablet and add it to the Home Screen.
  • Printing a register or curriculum sheetPrint Curriculum or a report from the right-hand panel of a course.
  • Printing your personal timetableMy Details, My Timetable, then Print inside the timetable window.
  • Data not showing the latest changes?The portal syncs every 15 minutes; ask an Admin for a manual sync.
  • Forgotten password or wrong username?Who to contact for credentials.

Termly learner ILP reviews

  • Termly learner ILP reviews: overviewHow termly reviews work and the set-up and review pages that follow.
  • Set up learner sign-in (no public signup)Create individual learner accounts, sync from the Google Sheet and what unlinked learners see.
  • Link an account to a learner recordPeople tab: verify the account and person code, then save the Learner account.
  • Assign an RG teacherChoose an RG teacher on the People tab and list exceptions.
  • Publish a termTerms tab: dates, template version, preview, publish, republishing and late work.
  • Edit a future template versionTemplates tab: clone a locked version, edit sections and questions, preview and publish.
  • Review submissions (RG teachers)ILP Tutor Reviews: open a submission, write a comment, complete or return it.
  • Start the next academic yearPublish template changes and the new year's terms; earlier years stay as read-only history.
  • Short guide for learnersSign in, answer questions, save a draft and submit to your tutor.
