Getting started

Staff roles

What to look for

  1. Admin and HR. Full access, including users, roles, all data and sync settings.
  2. Head of Dept. Department timetable, all courses, read-only user list, edit course timetable slots.
  3. Staff and Office. Staff: own timetable, courses, attendance, My Details. Office: attendance overview, registers, reports.

Every staff member is assigned a role that controls what they can see and do in the portal.

RoleWho has itAccess level
AdminSenior leadership, IT administratorsFull access — manage users, roles, all data, and sync settings
HRHuman Resources staffFull access equivalent to Admin, including staff records
Head of DeptHeads of DepartmentDepartment timetable, all courses, read-only user list; can edit course timetable slots
StaffTeaching and support staffPersonal timetable, own course data, attendance marking, My Details
OfficeOffice and admin staffAttendance overview, registers, reports — no course editing
Check before relying on this page. The current portal assigns the roles Admin, Head of Dept, Staff and Office. The HR row is kept from the original guide but HR is not a separate role in the current portal; confirm with the Admin Team.
