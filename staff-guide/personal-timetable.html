Daily work

My Timetable

What to look for

  1. My Details in the account menu. Open your account menu at the top right and choose My details, then My Timetable.
  2. Mon-Fri weekly grid. Each lesson sits on the time axis at its actual time.
  3. Time axis 08:30-15:30. Read the start and end of each lesson from the left-hand times.

From My Details, tap the My Timetable button to see a full Mon–Fri calendar view of your personal weekly schedule. Lessons are shown on a live time axis (08:30–15:30) so you can see exactly when each lesson falls. Shared Morning Meetings for Head of Department entries appear automatically for all HoD users.
