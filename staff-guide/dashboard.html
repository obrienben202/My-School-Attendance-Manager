Daily work

Homepage dashboard

What to look for

  1. Dashboard in the sidebar. Select it at any time to return to the homepage.
  2. Snapshot area. Today's schedule, attendance headlines, upcoming lessons and at-risk learner alerts.
  3. Attention bell. Opens work that needs attention.

The homepage gives a quick snapshot the moment you log in — today's schedule, attendance headlines, upcoming lessons, and any at-risk learner alerts.

Check before relying on this page. Written for the 2024/25 guide. The dashboard contents available to your role may differ today.
