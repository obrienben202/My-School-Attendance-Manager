Courses

My Courses

View every course you teach. Click any course card to open the Course Detail page, which shows:

  • Enrolled LearnersFull register of students on the course with attendance percentages, year group, and student reference numbers.
  • Timetable SlotsAll scheduled lesson times for that course across the week. Admin and HoD can add, edit, or remove slots.
  • Assessment PlanAssessments linked to the course — type, due date, grading scheme, and mandatory status.
  • Reports & RegistersPrint or export the Course Register, Attendance Analysis, Completion Report, Retention Statistics, and Grade Distribution in one click.
  • Course DetailsLevel, Faculty, Academic Year, Course Hours, Status — all pulled automatically from the school's live Google Sheet.
  • ProgressionsTrack individual learner progression routes and outcomes linked to each course.
