1Signing in

Choose Staff or Learner first, then enter the username and password issued by the school. There is no self sign-up and no password reset in the portal; ask the data office.

Sessions expire. When that happens you return to this card and any unsaved screen data is cleared. Sign out on shared computers; closing the tab also ends the stored session.

2Finding your way

Staff see a sidebar listing only the areas your role allows, grouped by section. On a phone, press Menu at the top left. Learners see a simple tab bar with their own pages only; no staff menus are ever shown to learners. Help always opens in a new tab from the top bar.

3Taking a register

  1. Open Take register, enter the Schedule ID of the lesson and the date, then press Open register.
  2. Pick a status for every learner: Present, Absent, Late, Authorised, or N/M (Not Marked). Mark unmarked as Present fills the remaining rows.
  3. Add short notes if needed, check the tally, then press Submit register.
Registers are only saved when you press Submit register. Leaving the page before submitting keeps your marks for this browser session only.

4Editing a record

Open an area from the menu, search (results come from the server, 25 per page), and choose a row. Fields marked * are required. Fields labelled Linked record ID point to another record. JSON fields must be valid JSON; errors show beneath the field. Archive asks for confirmation and hides the record from lists; it is not permanently deleted.

5When someone else saved first

If a colleague saved the same record after you opened it, your save is stopped and your draft is kept. The portal shows both versions side by side.

Read the saved version before choosing option 1. Saving your draft afterwards replaces your colleague's changes.

6Files

Where your role allows, records show a Files panel. Upload PDF, JPEG or PNG files up to 2 MB. Downloads are produced inside your browser; no shareable links are created. Do not upload files you would not want visible to everyone with access to that record.

7Learner reviews

Learners open a review from their tabs, write answers, summary and targets (one per line), then press Save draft as often as they like. Submit to tutor asks for confirmation and makes the review read only. Learners cannot change staff comments. Staff add shared comments from the review record; those comments are visible to the learner.

8Reports and CSV

Authorised roles can run attendance, assessment and enrolment reports and export them as CSV. Cells that begin with characters a spreadsheet would treat as a formula are prefixed with an apostrophe for safety.

Exported CSV files contain learner information. Store them only in approved school locations and delete local copies when finished.

9Setup notes for administrators

  1. Deploy the web app from the Apps Script project; share the deployed URL only with school users.
  2. Accounts are created by administrators in the configured data store. Never share sign-in details by email in plain text.
  3. If staff see "Google connection unavailable", they have opened the files outside the Apps Script deployment. Send them the deployed URL.
  4. Configuration errors are reported as "not fully configured"; check the project settings rather than editing spreadsheets by hand.
Do not edit revision or ID columns directly in the spreadsheet. Doing so breaks conflict protection and can overwrite colleagues' work.

All names, IDs and figures in this guide are synthetic examples.
