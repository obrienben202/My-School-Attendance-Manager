---
name: Termly ILP staff comments
description: User-selected staff-wide commenting scope and its privacy boundary.
---

Any signed-in school staff member may comment on a learner's termly ILP, including office staff. These are separate staff-only notes, not replacements for the assigned RG teacher's comment. This access does not grant permission to open private learner answers.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected "Any signed-in staff member" for the Staff button. The existing learner-answer and RG-teacher review restrictions remain separate from that choice.

**How to apply:** Preserve staff-wide commenting when changing permissions or navigation, but keep learner work and teacher review access scoped as before. Independent staff notes must not invalidate an in-progress learner save merely by changing the learner-work revision.
