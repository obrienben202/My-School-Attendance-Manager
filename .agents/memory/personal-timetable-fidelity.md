---
name: Personal timetable fidelity
description: Preserve recorded timetable sessions when presenting them in the reference school grid.
---

Treat the reference school periods as a presentation template, not evidence that a staff session follows those periods. Do not infer assignments, merge duplicate-looking records, or change times to make the grid look tidier.

**Why:** The user explicitly required that no real session be discarded or retimed; the original timetable includes activities and actual teaching times that differ from the reference grid.

**How to apply:** Cross-period teaching may appear in each affected lesson cell with its complete recorded range. If a session overlaps registration, break or lunch, keep the entire record in a labelled additional section rather than suggesting the part shown in lesson cells is its complete duration. Apply the same fidelity rule to print output.
