---
name: Staff guide image fidelity
description: Staff guide structure, new-tab entry points and privacy-safe illustration fidelity.
---

Staff-guide images must show recognisable, current portal controls. Numbered callouts must point to the element described, not generic fixed locations. Do not invent buttons that the real page does not offer.

**Why:** The user wants images so staff know what they are looking for. Generic placeholder screens and incorrectly anchored numbers undermine that purpose.

**How to apply:** Compare the relevant current screen or source labels before creating or updating illustrations, especially attendance marking, administration tabs and save controls.

Every separate standalone guide topic must include a relevant labelled image and explanations of what staff should look for. Preserve the images when splitting or restyling the guide.

**Why:** The user repeated the need for pictures after the standalone guide was rebuilt without them.

**How to apply:** Check every reader-facing topic, not just whether image assets exist elsewhere in the app. Images must be readable or enlargeable on phones as well as desktops.

Use generic illustrated examples, clearly labelled as such, rather than publishing live learner, safeguarding or applicant records in static guide assets. Properly anonymised screenshots can also be used when they are verified safe.

**Why:** Training assets must not expose private school records, and an illustration must not be mistaken for a live screenshot.

**How to apply:** Check image contents independently of the guide's access controls; static image URLs can be read without signing in.

The learner sign-in setup guide is for student accounts only. Do not include a staff login sheet link.

**Why:** The user says staff already have their own logins and requested only the student login sheet in this guide.

**How to apply:** Keep student onboarding separate from staff accounts when editing the learner setup instructions or illustrations.

The staff Help button and Staff Help Guide link should open the standalone staff guide in a new tab, rather than the portal's illustrated React help pages.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the previous guide and new-tab behaviour.

**How to apply:** Keep the guide separate from the staff member's working portal tab. Do not replace these entry points with the portal's illustrated help pages unless the user asks.

The standalone guide should have a clear contents/search page and a separate page for each subject, including individual tips and termly ILP tasks. Do not show all instructions in one long page.

**Why:** The user wants staff to find what they need without loading a page full of everything.

**How to apply:** Preserve coverage of the original instructions when reorganising the guide; split by the task the reader is looking for, not just by broad section headings.
